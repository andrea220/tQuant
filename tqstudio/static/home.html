tqstudio

Equity derivatives pricing and risk on the tQuant engine: prices are TensorFlow graphs, so the greeks (delta, vega per surface node, rho per curve pillar) come from automatic differentiation, through the model calibrations. Market data come from the latest snapshot; the engine runs on this machine and the pages are opened from any browser on the network.

Pages

Market data
One underlying at a glance: spot, repo and dividends, forwards and implied dividend yield, discount curve, implied volatility surface (smiles, ATM term structure), correlations.
Tradepad
Define a vanilla, digital or barrier option and price it with Black-Scholes (closed forms, digitals as a spread on the smile) or local vol (Monte Carlo): price, delta, vega, rho and the vega per surface node.
