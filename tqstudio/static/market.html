Fit to the listed options

The out-of-the-money options used to build the surface (puts below the forward, calls above), one per listed expiry and strike. Market: mid of bid / ask (hover for bid / ask; s = settlement price, no two-sided quote). Quote Time: time of the last bid update (terminal time zone), or its date when it is of an earlier session (orange). Black-Scholes: the option repriced on this snapshot (spot, dividends, repo, discount curve; American for single stocks). Surface Valuation: with the vol of the stored surface (the implied volatility surface table of the page), what the pricers read (bilinear between the nodes, flat beyond 50-150% of the spot). Model Valuation: with the vol of the lognormal-mixture smile fitted to the expiry, at the option's strike (the fit alone, no grid). Differences: Black-Scholes minus market; green inside bid / ask, red outside, grey for settlement prices.
