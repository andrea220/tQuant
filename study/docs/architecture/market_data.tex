\subsection{Snapshots on disk}\label{sec:arch-snapshots}
A snapshot is a folder \code{marketdata/<yyyymmdd>/<calibration\_set>/} (\code{calibration\_set} = time of the
snapshot, e.g.\ \code{2240}: several snapshots a day) with one CSV per data block; every file is optional.
Values are quotes, not objects: the library builds the market objects when loading.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.6cm}L{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
File & Columns \\
\midrule
\code{ir\_curves.csv} & \code{ccy, curve, pillar, value, quote, daycount, interp} (zero rates on pillar dates) \\
\code{ir\_vol.csv} & \code{ccy, curve, vol\_type, expiry, strike, vol} (caplet vols) \\
\code{ir\_swaption\_vol.csv} & \code{ccy, curve, expiry, tenor, strike\_offset, vol, vol\_type} \\
\code{eq\_spot.csv} & \code{ticker, ccy, spot, repo, div\_yield} \\
\code{eq\_dividends.csv} & \code{ticker, ccy, ex\_date, pay\_date, amount} \\
\code{eq\_vol.csv} & \code{ticker, ccy, expiry, strike, vol} (Black vols on absolute strikes) \\
\code{fx\_spot.csv}, \code{fx\_vol.csv} & \code{base, quote, spot}; \code{base, quote, expiry, strike, vol} \\
\code{correlations.csv} & \code{factor\_1, factor\_2, corr} (pairs; factor = key prefix, e.g.\ \code{EQ:EUR:TICKER1}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\code{MarketEnvironment.from\_folder} (arguments: the root folder, the date and the calibration set) loads
the latest snapshot when the last two are \code{None}; the evaluation date is the folder date. \code{from\_frames} builds the market
from the tables, \code{to\_frames} / \code{to\_folder} write it back (exact round trip). The synthetic snapshots
come from \code{tq.generate\_market\_data} (tables) / \code{tq.generate\_market} (in memory), seeded:
Nelson--Siegel OIS curves (EUR, GBP, USD) and EUR 6M as ESTR + spread, SSVI equity and FX smiles, caplet vols and
swaption cube from one G2++, 50 tickers with discrete dividends, correlations.
