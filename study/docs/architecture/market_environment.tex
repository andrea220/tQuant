\subsection{Snapshots on disk}\label{sec:arch-snapshots}\label{sec:arch-market-data}
A snapshot is a folder \code{marketdata/<yyyymmdd>/<calibration\_set>/} (\code{calibration\_set} = time of the
snapshot, e.g.\ \code{2240}: several snapshots a day) with one CSV per data block; every file is optional.
Values are quotes, not objects: the library builds the market objects when loading.

\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.6cm}L{11.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
File & Columns \\
\midrule

\code{ir\_vol.csv} & \code{ccy, curve, vol\_type, expiry, strike, vol} (caplet vols) \\
\code{ir\_swaption\_vol.csv} & \code{ccy, curve, expiry, tenor, strike\_offset, vol, vol\_type} \\
\code{eq\_spot.csv} & \code{ticker, ccy, spot, repo, div\_yield} \\

\code{eq\_vol.csv} & \code{ticker, ccy, expiry, strike, vol} (Black vols on absolute strikes) \\
\code{fx\_spot.csv}, \code{fx\_vol.csv} & \code{base, quote, spot}; \code{base, quote, expiry, strike, vol} \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\code{MarketEnvironment.from\_folder} (arguments: the root folder, the date and the calibration set) loads
the latest snapshot when the last two are \code{None}; the evaluation date is the folder date. \code{from\_frames} builds the market
from the tables, \code{to\_frames} / \code{to\_folder} write it back (exact round trip). The synthetic snapshots
come from \code{tq.generate\_market\_data} (tables) / \code{tq.generate\_market} (in memory), seeded:
Nelson--Siegel OIS curves (EUR, GBP, USD) and EUR 6M as ESTR + spread, SSVI equity and FX smiles, caplet vols and
swaption cube from one G2++, 50 tickers with discrete dividends, correlations.
\nb{market\_handles.ipynb}

\subsection{MarketEnvironment and keys}\label{sec:arch-market-env}
\code{MarketEnvironment} is the only access point to market data (pricers never read files or hold their own
copies). It is a flat dictionary with keys \code{RISKFACTOR:CCY:TICKER:TYPE}:
\code{RISKFACTOR} $\in$ \{\code{IR}, \code{EQ}, \code{FX}, \code{CORR}\} (enum \code{RiskFactor}),
\code{TYPE} $\in$ \{\code{SPOT}, \code{VOL}, \code{SWVOL}, \code{REPO}, \code{DIVYIELD}, \code{DIV}\} (enum
\code{MarketDataType}); \code{MarketMapValidator} checks the format and the type of each value.

\begin{tabular}{@{}L{4.4cm}L{4.3cm}L{6.1cm}@{}}
\toprule
Key & Value & Accessor \\
\midrule
\code{IR:EUR:ESTR:SPOT} & \code{RateCurve} & \code{get\_ir\_curve(ccy, ticker=None)} (default: OIS) \\
\code{IR:EUR:6M:VOL} / \code{:SWVOL} & caplet \code{VolatilitySurface} / \code{SwaptionVolCube} & \code{get\_ir\_vol\_surface},

\subsection{Market objects and risk factors}\label{sec:arch-risk-factors}
Every market input is a float64 \code{tf.Variable} owned by a market object, with a stable identity (updates by
\code{assign}, so tapes and references stay valid). \code{market\_env.risk\_factors()} returns
\code{\{key: [variables]\}}: one \code{tape.gradient(price, market\_env.risk\_factors())} gives every bucketed
sensitivity at once.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{RateCurve}: pillars (dates or year fractions, Act/365 in the snapshots), one variable per pillar zero
        rate (\code{rates}, \code{variables}); \code{discount}, \code{zero\_rate}, \code{forward\_rate},
        \code{inst\_fwd} (definitions: Models and Pricing, Curves). Interpolation \code{LINEAR} through
        \code{numericalhandles.LinearInterp}.
  \item \code{VolatilitySurface}: a grid of absolute strikes (\code{strike}) and maturities in years
        (\code{maturity}); the vols are one variable, \code{volatility\_matrix} of shape $[n_T, n_K]$, read by
        \code{volatility(strike, tenor)}; \code{vol\_type} is lognormal (equity, FX) or normal (caplets).
  \item \code{DividendCurve}: fixed ex-dates and payment dates, the amounts as one variable; PV with a discount curve.
  \item Scalars (spot, repo, dividend yield, FX spot): one variable each; \code{MarketEnvironment} wraps a float
        into a variable.
  \item \code{SwaptionVolCube}, \code{CorrelationMatrix}: one variable each (cube; $[n, n]$ matrix).
\end{itemize}
\nb{market\_handles.ipynb}

\subsection{The Equity view}\label{sec:arch-equity}
\code{market\_env.get\_equity(ticker)} returns an \code{Equity}: references (not copies) to spot, repo, dividend
yield, dividends, vol surface and the discount curve of the currency, plus \code{currency} and \code{ticker}. It is
the single place for the forward (\code{forward}, \code{pv\_dividends}, \code{implied\_div\_yield}) and the entry
point of the model factories (\code{from\_equity}). Dividend model from the data: \code{DIV} present $\Rightarrow$
\code{"discrete"} (\code{DIVYIELD} ignored), otherwise \code{"continuous"}.
\nb{market\_handles.ipynb} \nb{equity\_underlying.ipynb}

\code{DividendCurve.pv\_dividends(T, curve)} sums the amounts with ex-date in $(t_0, T]$, each discounted at its
\emph{payment date} (the ex-date when the schedule has no payment dates), with dense weights (a dense gradient on the
amounts vector). In the synthetic data the payment is 14 days after the ex-date.

\paragraph{Where the data come from (live snapshots).} \code{marketdata/mdbuild} downloads them from the Bloomberg
Desktop API (fields in \code{marketdata/config/settings.yaml}); single stocks (\code{Equity} yellow key) and equity
indices (\code{Index} yellow key, listed under \code{equity\_indices} in \code{universe.yaml}) differ in the dividends,
the options and, for SPX, the spot.

(\code{calibration/equityvol.py}): implied forward and repo per expiry, a smile per expiry, the surface on the
listed expiries. The method (filters, de-Americanization, repo, lognormal-mixture smiles, interpolation and
extrapolation) is in \emph{Models and Pricing}, Equity volatilities, Construction from listed options.

\subsection{Interest-rate curves: data and bootstrap}\label{sec:arch-ir-data}
The curves are bootstrapped from the members of Bloomberg curves (ICVS), listed in \code{universe.yaml} under
\code{curves} as \code{"<CCY> <name>": <ticker>}: EUR ESTR \code{YCSW0514 Index}, EUR 6M \code{YCSW0045 Index},
USD SOFR \code{YCSW0490 Index}, GBP SONIA \code{YCSW0141 Index}.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{2.6cm}L{12.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
Data & Source \\
\midrule
Curve quotes & bulk field \code{CURVE\_TENOR\_RATES} of the curve ticker: per member its tenor, ticker and
  \code{Mid Yield} (in \%, stored in decimals); the member is the index fixing (an \code{Index} ticker), an OIS, an FRA
  (Ibor curves, up to 18M) or a swap; \code{ir\_quotes.csv} (\code{ccy, curve, tenor, instrument, quote, bloomberg,
  last\_update}) \\
Fixings & \code{PX\_LAST} (in \%) and \code{LAST\_UPDATE\_DT} of \code{ESTRON}, \code{EUR006M}, \code{SOFRRATE},
  \code{SONIO/N} (\code{Index}); \code{ir\_fixings.csv} \\
Rate vols & not downloaded (caplet vols and swaption cube: internal service, to come) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\code{bootstrap\_from\_quotes} (\code{markethandles/bootstrapping.py}) builds one instrument per quote with the
conventions of \code{QUOTE\_CONVENTIONS} (overnight curves first, the Ibor one discounted on its overnight curve), solves
the pillar rates with \code{newton} on an AAD Jacobian and registers \code{RateCurve}s with \code{MONOTONE\_CONVEX}
interpolation, each with \code{jacobian}, \code{calib\_quote\_dv01} and \code{calibration\_quotes}.
\code{bootstrap\_snapshot} runs it right after the download and writes \code{ir\_curves.csv} (zero rates on the pillar
dates), \code{ir\_curves\_jacobian.csv} and \code{ir\_calibration.csv}; \code{from\_folder} then reads the curves
without bootstrapping (a snapshot with quotes only is bootstrapped when loaded). Conventions, interpolation and the
par sensitivities: \emph{Models and Pricing}, Interest-rate curves (Monotone convex interpolation, Bootstrap from
market quotes).

\subsection{FX: data}\label{sec:arch-fx-data}
FX pairs are \code{Curncy} tickers of the universe (\code{EURUSD}, \code{GBPUSD}, \code{EURGBP}, \code{EURCHF},
\code{USDJPY}): \code{PX\_LAST} gives the spot, 1 base = $x$ quote, stored in \code{fx\_spot.csv} (\code{base, quote,
spot}) and in the market as \code{FX:<quote>:<pair>:SPOT}; the daily \code{PX\_LAST} history (10 years) enters the
correlations. Forwards come from covered interest parity on the default curves of the two currencies
(\code{get\_fx\_forward}): a pair whose currency has no curve in the snapshot (CHF, JPY) has the spot only. FX vols:
historical for now (\code{mdbuild.history.historical\_fx\_vols}, the \code{fx\_vol} block of the settings: 52 weekly
log returns, annualised) as a flat surface in \code{fx\_vol.csv} (expiries 1M--10Y, strikes 50\%--200\% of the spot),
read as \code{FX:<quote>:<pair>:VOL}. \code{get\_quanto(ticker, ccy, payment\_ccy, tenor)}: the correlation of the
underlying with the value $X$ of one unit of \code{ccy} in \code{payment\_ccy} (the stored pair, or minus its
correlation for the inverse) and the vol of the pair, as tensors (AAD to the correlation matrix and the FX vols).

\paragraph{Historical fixings.} The closes of the equities before the snapshot date (\code{PX\_LAST} of the price
history, \code{mdbuild.history.equity\_fixings}; today's close is not known yet) in \code{eq\_fixings.csv}
(\code{ticker, ccy, date, value}), read as \code{EQ:<ccy>:<ticker>:FIXINGS}, a \code{Fixings} object (date $\to$
value; not a risk factor, absent from \code{risk\_factors()}); optional in \code{from\_frames} / \code{to\_frames}
(written only when present). Accessors \code{get\_eq\_fixings(ticker)} (\code{None} without) and
\code{get\_eq\_fixing(ticker, date)} (raises on a missing date: no fill from other dates).
