\subsection{Stochastic processes}\label{sec:arch-models}
\code{StochasticProcess} (\code{size}, \code{initial\_values}, \code{noise\_size}, \code{evolve}): the interface of
everything the Monte Carlo pricers simulate.

Common interface: \code{StochasticProcess} (\code{models/stochasticprocess.py}).
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{size()}: state dimension; \code{initial\_values()}; \code{noise\_size()}: normals per path and step
        (default 1);
  \item \code{evolve(t\_grid, dw, observation\_times=None)}:\\ it maps the pre-drawn normals \code{dw}
        ($[n_{\text{paths}}, n_{\text{steps}}]$, or with a trailing axis of size \code{noise\_size()}) to paths on the increasing
        grid \code{t\_grid} (without 0), returning only the \code{observation\_times} columns when given.
\end{itemize}
The caller draws the normals
(\code{tf.random.stateless\_normal} with a seed), which makes the paths reproducible and the bumps common-random-number
ones. \nb{monte\_carlo\_gbm.ipynb}

\subsection{Models and processes}\label{sec:arch-equity-models}
Equity model instances (\code{EquityModel}) separate from the market, bound to an \code{Equity} into a process.

An equity model (\code{models/equitymodel.py}) holds \emph{settings} (how it is built) and \emph{calibrated parameters}
(float64 \code{tf.Variable}s), never the market: the underlying is given to the calibration and to the binding.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{Model.calibrate(equity, **settings)}: an instance with the parameters, the fit \code{report}, the
        \code{ticker} and \code{calibration\_date}, and the calibration \code{jacobian}
        $\{\text{parameter}: \{\text{market key}: [\partial p / \partial\,\text{variable}]\}\}$ with the keys of
        \code{Equity.risk\_factors()} (computed by \code{tape.jacobian} through the differentiable calibration);
        \code{Model(**parameters)} by hand: no Jacobian;
  \item \code{model.process(equity)}: a new \code{StochasticProcess} (\code{XxxProcess}, the model is not modified)
        that rebuilds the market-dependent part (forwards, local vol grid, leverage) and simulates; the closed forms
        (\code{vanilla\_price}, \code{implied\_vol}) live on the process;
  \item \code{Model.from\_equity(equity, maturity, strike)}: a process calibrated inside the caller's tape (greeks
        through the calibration), the factory the pricers use when given the class;
  \item \code{model.parameters} (model greeks);\\
        \code{par\_greeks(model, model\_greeks, market\_greeks)}
        ($\partial V/\partial m = \partial V/\partial m|_{\text{direct}} + \sum_p \partial V/\partial p\,\partial p/\partial m$),
        \code{to\_frames()} / \code{from\_frames()} (settings, parameters, Jacobian in long format, report: exact round
        trip; JSON values for the settings).
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{llll}
  \toprule
  model & settings & parameters & process \\
  \midrule
  \code{LocalVolatilityModel} & grid, wings, $g$ floor & none (vegas direct) & \code{LocalVolatilityProcess} \\
  \code{HestonModel} & martingale correction & $v_0, \kappa, \theta, \xi, \rho$ & \code{HestonProcess} \\
  \code{DisplacedDiffusionModel} & slice parameters & $\beta, \sigma$ per piece & \code{DisplacedDiffusionProcess} \\
  \code{HestonSLVModel} & mixing, smoother, cap & none (Heston held) & \code{HestonSLVProcess} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\code{HestonSLVModel} composes a \code{HestonModel} and a \code{LocalVolatilityModel}; its tables nest theirs
(\code{heston.*}, \code{local\_vol.*}). \code{GeometricBrownianMotion} stays a process with an option-specific
\code{from\_equity} (vol at $(K, T)$).

The Monte Carlo pricers (\code{VanillaMCPricer}, \code{AutocallableMCPricer}) take a model class or an instance
(\code{check\_model}, \code{bind}): a class is calibrated at each price inside the tape (\code{from\_equity}); an
instance is bound with \code{process(equity)}, its ticker checked against the product's underlying. One backward pass
on a nested structure gives model and market greeks:
\begin{center}
  \code{pricer.tape.gradient(price, \{"model": params, "market": factors\})},
\end{center}
with \code{params = model.parameters} and \code{factors = equity.risk\_factors()}. The pricers
simulate on a uniform grid ending at the expiry and leave \code{standard\_error}, \code{discount\_factor} and
\code{time\_to\_maturity} on the product. \nb{heston\_slv.ipynb}

\subsection{Calibration}\label{sec:arch-calibration}
Calibration inside the factories (implicit-function greeks), the stored Jacobian, and the rates calibration module.

The least-squares calibrations (Heston, displaced diffusion) run in NumPy / SciPy and end with one Newton step written
in TensorFlow on the optimality condition: the value stays at the optimum and the gradient is the implicit-function
one, $\mathrm{d}p/\mathrm{d}m = -H^{-1}\,\partial^2 L/\partial p\,\partial m$ (\code{\_implicit\_step}). Inside
\code{from\_equity} this makes the parameters differentiable functions of the market; \code{calibrate} evaluates the same
graph once and stores $\mathrm{d}p/\mathrm{d}m$ (\code{EquityModel.jacobian}), so that the par greeks of an instance
equal the greeks through the calibration (\code{tests/test\_model\_instances.py}). The rates calibration
(\code{calibration/}) is a separate module (least squares with an AAD Jacobian). \pending
