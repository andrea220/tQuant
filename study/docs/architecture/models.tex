\section{Models}\label{sec:arch-models-chapter}
The processes the Monte Carlo pricers simulate and the model instances that carry calibrated parameters. Dynamics,
closed forms and simulation schemes are in \emph{Models and Pricing}; how the models are calibrated and stored is in
Section~\ref{sec:arch-calibration-chapter}.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Stochastic processes}\label{sec:arch-models}
\code{StochasticProcess} (\code{size}, \code{initial\_values}, \code{noise\_size}, \code{evolve}): the interface of
everything the Monte Carlo pricers simulate.

Common interface: \code{StochasticProcess} (\code{models/stochasticprocess.py}).
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{size()}: state dimension; \code{initial\_values()}; \code{noise\_size()}: normals per path and step
        (default 1);
  \item \code{evolve(t\_grid, dw, observation\_times=None)}:\\ it maps the pre-drawn normals \code{dw}
        ($[n_{\text{paths}}, n_{\text{steps}}]$, or with a trailing axis of size \code{noise\_size()}) to paths on the increasing
        grid \code{t\_grid} (without 0), returning only the \code{observation\_times} columns when given.
\end{itemize}
The caller draws the normals
(\code{tf.random.stateless\_normal} with a seed), which makes the paths reproducible and the bumps common-random-number
ones. \nb{monte\_carlo\_gbm.ipynb}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\paragraph{\code{MultiAssetProcess} (\code{multiasset.py}).} $n$ single-asset processes with one normal per step
(local vol, GBM) and an $[n, n]$ correlation tensor: \code{evolve(t\_grid, dw)} with \code{dw}
\code{[n\_paths, n\_steps, n]} independent normals, correlated by the Cholesky factor ($z = dw\,L^\top$), column $k$
to process $k$; paths \code{[n\_paths, n\_steps, n]}, differentiable w.r.t. the correlation. Used by the worst-of
autocallable.

\subsection{Equity models and processes}\label{sec:arch-equity-models}
Equity model instances (\code{EquityModel}) separate from the market, bound to an \code{Equity} into a process.

An equity model (\code{models/equitymodel.py}) holds \emph{settings} (how it is built) and \emph{calibrated parameters}
(float64 \code{tf.Variable}s), never the market: the underlying is given to the calibration and to the binding.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{Model.calibrate(equity, **settings)}: an instance with the parameters, the fit \code{report}, the
        \code{ticker} and \code{calibration\_date}, and the calibration \code{jacobian}
        $\{\text{parameter}: \{\text{market key}: [\partial p / \partial\,\text{variable}]\}\}$ with the keys of
        \code{Equity.risk\_factors()} (computed by \code{tape.jacobian} through the differentiable calibration);
        \code{Model(**parameters)} by hand: no Jacobian;
  \item \code{model.process(equity)}: a new \code{StochasticProcess} (\code{XxxProcess}, the model is not modified)
        that rebuilds the market-dependent part (forwards, local vol grid, leverage) and simulates; the closed forms
        (\code{vanilla\_price}, \code{implied\_vol}) live on the process;
  \item \code{Model.from\_equity(equity, maturity, strike)}: a process calibrated inside the caller's tape (greeks
        through the calibration), the factory the pricers use when given the class;
  \item \code{model.parameters} (model greeks);\\
        \code{par\_greeks(model, model\_greeks, market\_greeks, equity)}
        ($\partial V/\partial m = \partial V/\partial m|_{\text{direct}} + \sum_p \partial V/\partial p\,\partial p/\partial m$),
        \code{to\_frames()} / \code{from\_frames()} (settings, parameters, Jacobian in long format, report: exact round
        trip; JSON values for the settings).
\end{itemize}

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{llll}
  \toprule
  model & settings & parameters & process \\
  \midrule
  \code{LocalVolatilityModel} & grid, wings, $g$ floor & none (vegas direct) & \code{LocalVolatilityProcess} \\
  \code{HestonModel} & martingale correction & $v_0, \kappa, \theta, \xi, \rho$ & \code{HestonProcess} \\
  \code{DisplacedDiffusionModel} & slice parameters & $\beta, \sigma$ per piece & \code{DisplacedDiffusionProcess} \\
  \code{HestonSLVModel} & mixing, cap, particles, nodes & $\E[v \mid X]$ table (Heston held) & \code{HestonSLVProcess} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\code{HestonSLVModel} composes a \code{HestonModel} and a \code{LocalVolatilityModel}; its tables nest theirs
(\code{heston.*}, \code{local\_vol.*}) plus the nodes of the leverage table. \code{calibrate\_leverage(equity)} (the
daily step) runs \code{calibrate\_expectation} (particles of its own, normals seeded per row, \code{tf.function}) and
stores $\E[v \mid X]$ (parameter \code{expectation}, rows $\times$ nodes) and the nodes (\code{leverage\_table});
with \code{bias\_correction} (default) a second run on the same nodes, Richardson-corrected and outside the tape, is
stored as \code{leverage\_table["price\_values"]} (frame \code{leverage\_price\_values}). \code{process} reads them
(one-hot row picks: dense gradient to the table) and \code{HestonSLVProcess.simulate} returns
\code{X\_risk + stop\_gradient(X\_price - X\_risk)}: the value of the corrected table, the derivative of the
parameter (smoothing for risk). The calibration and pricing steps are wrapped in \code{tf.recompute\_grad}
(check-pointing: only the state $(v, \ln X)$ per step on the tape, the step recomputed in the backward pass; 1Y put
with AAD about 8~s instead of 25--40~s). \code{map\_model\_greeks} (called by
\code{par\_greeks}, which then needs the \code{equity}) is the adjoint: the calibration re-run on the stored nodes
inside a tape, up to the last row with a non-zero $\partial V/\partial \E$, one \code{tape.gradient} with \code{output\_gradients}; the
default \code{EquityModel.map\_model\_greeks} uses the stored Jacobian. \code{HestonSLVModel.from\_equity} calibrates
Heston and the table inside the pricing tape (same greeks in one pass; tests in \code{tests/test\_slv\_vega.py}). \code{GeometricBrownianMotion} stays a process with an option-specific
\code{from\_equity} (vol at $(K, T)$).

The Monte Carlo pricers (\code{VanillaMCPricer}, \code{AutocallableMCPricer}) take a model class or an instance
(\code{check\_model}, \code{bind}): a class is calibrated at each price inside the tape (\code{from\_equity}); an
instance is bound with \code{process(equity)}, its ticker checked against the product's underlying. One backward pass
on a nested structure gives model and market greeks:
\begin{center}
  \code{pricer.tape.gradient(price, \{"model": params, "market": factors\})},
\end{center}
with \code{params = model.parameters} and \code{factors = equity.risk\_factors()}. The pricers
simulate on a uniform grid ending at the expiry and leave \code{standard\_error}, \code{discount\_factor} and
\code{time\_to\_maturity} on the product. \nb{heston\_slv.ipynb}

\subsection{The equity models}\label{sec:arch-equity-model-list}
All simulate $X_t = S_t/F(t)$ on the market forward of the \code{Equity} view (\code{equity.forward(t)}: spot, repo,
dividends, curve) and return $S_t = F(t)\,X_t$, so every model reprices the forward at every date; with
\code{martingale\_correction} (default) the simulated $X_t$ is rescaled to sample mean 1 at each time.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \code{GeometricBrownianMotion}, \code{ArithmeticBrownianMotion} (\code{brownian.py}): processes only, no
        \code{EquityModel}; \code{GeometricBrownianMotion.from\_equity(equity, maturity, strike)} is the Black--Scholes
        model of one option (vol of the surface at $(K, T)$, spot net of the dividends to $T$), right for a single
        expiry only. \code{observation\_columns} picks the observation times on a grid.
  \item \code{LocalVolatilityModel} / \code{LocalVolatilityProcess} (\code{localvolatility.py}): Dupire--Gatheral local vol
        (\code{gatheral\_local\_vol}) on the market nodes in forward log-moneyness, on a grid of \code{n\_y} points
        (300) and time steps of \code{time\_step} (1/96) up to the product's maturity, wings capped
        (\code{max\_wing\_slope}), $g$ floored (\code{g\_floor}, an arbitrage warning when the surface violates it); no
        calibrated parameter: the vegas go straight to the vol nodes.
  \item \code{DisplacedDiffusionModel} / \code{DisplacedDiffusionProcess} (\code{displaceddiffusion.py}): shifted
        lognormal with piecewise constant forward $\beta, \sigma$ (\code{t\_nodes}, \code{beta\_fwd},
        \code{sigma\_fwd}); closed form \code{displaced\_diffusion\_price}, slice parameters turned into forward pieces
        by \code{forward\_from\_slices}; exact Monte Carlo steps.
  \item \code{HestonModel} / \code{HestonProcess} (\code{heston.py}): parameters $v_0, \kappa, \theta, \xi, \rho$;
        \code{heston\_characteristic\_function}, \code{heston\_price} (COS), \code{heston\_cumulants}; QE-M Monte Carlo,
        \code{noise\_size() = 2}; \code{feller} on the process.
  \item \code{HestonSLVModel} / \code{HestonSLVProcess} (\code{hestonslv.py}): a \code{HestonModel} (vol of vol scaled by
        \code{mixing}), a \code{LocalVolatilityModel} and the leverage $\sigma_{\mathrm{LV}}/\sqrt{\E[v \mid X]}$ with
        $\E[v \mid X]$ calibrated by \code{calibrate\_expectation} (particle method) and stored as the parameter
        \code{expectation}; details below.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Rates models}\label{sec:arch-rates-models}
\code{GaussianShortRateModel(curve, kappas, sigmas, correlation=None, sigma\_times=None)}
(\code{gaussianshortrate.py}): $n$-factor Gaussian short rate (G1++ = Hull--White, G2++) on a discount curve, the
shift $\varphi(t)$ fitting $P(0,\cdot)$ exactly; vols constant or piecewise constant on \code{sigma\_times}. It is a
\code{StochasticProcess} (exact joint simulation of the factors and their integrals) with closed forms: bonds
$P(t,T)$ on the paths, the path discount $D(0,t)$, caplets (\code{caplet}), swaptions (\code{swaption}: Gaussian
swap-rate approximation or exact, Jamshidian / Brigo--Mercurio). The curve enters only through $P(0,\cdot)$, so
prices are differentiable in its pillars. Not split into model and process yet (its parameters are given to the
constructor); used by the rates calibration (Section~\ref{sec:arch-rates-calibration}) and the swap exposures
(\code{SwapExposureGenerator}, \code{analytics/}).
