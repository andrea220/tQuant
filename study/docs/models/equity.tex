\subsection{Implied volatility surface}\label{sec:mp-vol-surface}
Market quotes are Black implied vols $\sigma_{ij}$ on maturities $T_i$ (years) $\times$ absolute strikes $K_j$. The
surface used by the pricers (\code{VolatilitySurface.volatility(K, T)}) is bilinear in $(K, T)$ on the vols and flat
outside the grid: a vol between nodes depends on the 4 surrounding nodes only, so the vega of a price is bucketed
on the nodes (gradient w.r.t.\ the matrix). The interpolation is not arbitrage-free by construction (linear in vol,
not in total variance); the local vol model builds its own smooth representation in total variance and forward
moneyness (\S\ref{sec:mp-lv}). In the synthetic data the smiles come from an SSVI parametrisation (arbitrage-free on
the nodes): steep negative skew at short maturities, flattening with $T$.
\nb{01\_market\_handles.ipynb}
